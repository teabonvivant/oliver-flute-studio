# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

張老師音樂學院 is a public Hong Kong Traditional Chinese music education website. The user wants visitors to find it professional, trustworthy, stylish and culturally expressive.

## Users

Existing content addresses adults beginning or returning to music, parents of children learning music, and students developing performance or exam skills. These audiences are inferred directly from the existing named pages; no new audience is being introduced.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The current request authorizes a frontend visual redesign while preserving existing content. Keep all 200 articles, 19 page routes, copy, images, contact details and destinations. Preserve search, topic filters, sorting, pagination, course guidance, adult preference selection, practice checklist persistence, print and WhatsApp message preparation.

The existing implementation is static HTML generated by Node.js, vanilla CSS and JavaScript, with a small Sites Worker for redirects and fallback. Publish to the existing public Sites project. Do not replace the stack or the source repository.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the name 張老師音樂學院, 張家詠 / Oliver Cheung, the original logo and teacher portrait, the authored Traditional Chinese copy, and the existing illustration collection. The user rejected the cobalt and oversized poster direction. The approved identity is classical, artistic, literary and Japanese minimalist, specifically the book-like composition of AMAHARE and the restrained whitespace of SABOE. Execute that direction throughout desktop and mobile, with quiet typography and readable content.

## Evidence on Hand

The website contains verified teacher information and contact details, original teacher and logo assets, nine generated editorial illustrations and 200 article-specific instructional diagrams. No new claims, pricing, credentials, testimonials or course availability are needed for this redesign.

## Product Principles

- Make the music and teaching content the subject of the visual design.
- Preserve all existing information and functionality.
- Give browsing and long-form reading different, coherent treatments.
- Make mobile navigation and keyboard interaction clear.
